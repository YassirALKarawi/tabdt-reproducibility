# TABDT reproducibility package v19

**Permanent archive:** [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21861066](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21861066)

This release archives the complete reproducibility package accompanying the
two-author manuscript "Trust and Age Bayesian Digital Twins: Provable
Predictive Maintenance under Industrial Network Impairments," prepared for
regular submission to IEEE Transactions on Industrial Cyber-Physical Systems
(TICPS).

## Authors

1. Yassir Ameen Al-Karawi - first author
2. Hamed Al-Raweshidy - second and corresponding author

## Included

- eight-page IEEE double-column manuscript and LaTeX source;
- deterministic synthetic simulator and machine-readable results;
- external XJTU-SY validation over all 15 physical bearings;
- vector PDF and 400-dpi PNG figures;
- integrity hashes, citation metadata, and file-specific rights information.

## Headline results

- up to 27.9% lower RUL RMSE in the synthetic study;
- XJTU-SY normalized RUL RMSE of 47.70 for TABDT versus 52.11 and 53.02 for
  the two delayed-packet baselines;
- external 90% interval coverage of 76.9%, reported as undercoverage rather
  than field calibration.

The 5.45 GB XJTU-SY raw archive and derived feature caches are intentionally
excluded.
